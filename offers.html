BMW M1 E36: €590,000

The BMW M1 E26 is a legendary sports car that holds a special place in automotive history. Launched in the late 1970s, it was BMW's first mid-engine car and marked the brand's entrance into the world of high-performance sports vehicles. Powered by a 3.5-liter inline-six engine, the M1 produces around 273 horsepower, accelerating from 0 to 60 mph in just over five seconds. Its lightweight construction enhances agility, making it a favorite among driving enthusiasts. Today, the M1 is celebrated for its blend of performance, design, and innovation.

AC Cobra: €670,000

The AC Cobra, one of the most iconic sports cars, was born from a collaboration between AC Cars and Carroll Shelby. Featuring a powerful V8 engine, the Cobra achieved remarkable performance, with impressive acceleration and handling. Its lightweight body and aggressive design contributed to its dominance in motorsport. The Cobra’s racing pedigree, combined with its enduring style, makes it a highly sought-after classic among car enthusiasts and collectors worldwide.
